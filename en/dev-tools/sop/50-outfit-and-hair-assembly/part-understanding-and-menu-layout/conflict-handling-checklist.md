# Conflict handling checklist

**Two layers**: items whose “Layer” column says **atlas** are gone through for every part when building the atlas, with conclusions written into the atlas's “conflict handling” column in the format “C0N: conclusion + evidence”; if not applicable, write “C0N: N/A + reason”. Items marked **project** (C01, C04, C05, C12, C15) depend on the specific avatar, other sets and the menu; written into the atlas they lose their reuse value. The atlas only carries one line “project layer → 60 step 3/5”, and they are done in the project ([60 Menu and animator layers](../../60-menu-and-animation-layers.md) step 3 fills the conflict table from screenshots, step 5 measures the delivered state after baking).
**IDs are fixed**: adding or removing items later does not renumber; new items continue from C16. D1–D8 in parentheses are the dependency kinds of perception declarations ([Scenario classification mapping](../../65-perception-mechanism/scenario-classification-reference.md) section 1), for easy transcription into the project's `_感知/声明.yaml`.

⚠ C08, C09, C11 involve body/foot blend shapes: neither the old projects' practice nor the regression methods **have been verified**; treat them only as samples that include both good and bad examples (`CLAUDE.md` division-of-labor section; [Foot-shape and shrink key ownership](../foot-and-shrink-key-ownership.md)). The atlas records “candidate keys + candidate host”, not conclusions.

## Skin-tight small items covering the nipple area go through three extra checks (candidate: derived from the C06 and C11 examples, pending H-03 verification)

**Identify the target by conditions, not by name**: dominant bone includes Breast (the measurement must be “skinning per vertex”, otherwise look at image A), image A is a small piece, and it is not a full bra.

- **Add it to the chest-cover gate's** explicit `when` list; if not added, it is visible while the nipple key is still written to 100 as if bare (`A/_感知/声明.yaml:605-619`). Old projects also had a practice of Deleting `Nipple_ON` (`lt/历史分析/D_形态键管理.md:55` 〔practice〕); Delete can't be undone and needs user confirmation ([Shrink key workflow and the two families](../shrink-key-workflow-and-families.md)).
- **Check bust keys**: does it have the keys the body will be driven with? If missing, add frames + Sync. Project D's Esmera nipple covers had problems at both ends: with `Breast_small` missing, the covers floated 4–8 mm off after the body's bust shrank (geometry measurement, `D/_施工记录.md:71,79`; earlier recorded from renders as “swallowed by the body, sunk into the skin”, `:53,55`, corrected by `:71`); with `Breast_big(limit)` missing, pushing the bust to the “big” side sank them into the skin (`:81`).
- **Textures are not downgraded on the grounds of “underwear is covered”**: page 80's reason for dropping the “underwear base color” to 1024 is “fully covered by outerwear in the vast majority of looks” ([Textures and PhysBones](../../80-performance-optimization/textures-and-physbones.md)), which doesn't hold for selling-point items; the user's red line is that easily visible main textures are not dropped to 1024 (same page `:41-42`).
